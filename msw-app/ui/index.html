Your desk, one click away

A profile remembers how your monitors are set up: which are on, where they sit, their resolution, and which is the main display. Save one for each way you use your desk and switch between them from the tray.

Your screens right now

Set up your screens in Windows

Arrange them the way you want this profile to be. The picture below updates as you go.

  • Drag the monitors to match where they sit on your desk.
  • To leave a monitor out, select it and choose Disconnect this display.
  • Tick Make this my main display on the one that should hold the taskbar.
  • Choose Keep changes when Windows asks.

Name it

Name it after when you use it: Work, Gaming, Laptop only.

Profile saved

Three ways to switch to it:

  • From the tray. Right-click the monitor icon by the clock and pick a profile. Look under the ^ arrow if it's hidden.
  • From this window. Click its tile under Profiles.
  • With a hotkey. Open the  menu on a tile and choose Set hotkey.

For another setup, rearrange your screens in Windows and choose Save current layout.
